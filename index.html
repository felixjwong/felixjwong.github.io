Solving aging: the fundamental question

Felix Wong

Thank you for visiting my homepage. I am co-founder and CEO of Integrated Bio. Founded in 2022, Integrated Bio is an aging science company developing multimodal biological datasets and AI for drug discovery, focusing on therapeutics for age-related diseases.

Previously, I was a PI supported by an NIH K25 Mentored Quantitative Research Career Development Award at The Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, where my lab was among the first to apply AI to drug discovery, resulting in the first AI-driven discoveries of classes of antibiotics and senolytics.
